# Short-form video scripts (TikTok / Reels / Shorts)

All numbers come from this repo's results. Each is about 30–45 seconds. Screen
recordings of the result tables in `reports/` work as B-roll.

---

## 1. "I tested the viral NQ strategy on 14 years of data"
**Hook (0–3s):** "This NQ strategy is all over my feed. So I made an AI test it on four point seven million candles."
**Body:** "I turned the rules into code: fair value at the 9:30 open, displacement candle, break of structure, one-and-a-half R target. Seventy-two versions. Real costs. Zero made money from 2012 to 2022. The best one since 2023? Basically break-even."
**Close:** "The rules aren't the edge. Full results in my bio."

## 2. "A 97% win rate that loses money"
**Hook:** "This strategy won 97% of the time. It lost money."
**Body:** "On Polymarket, buying the near-certain side wins almost every bet, but each win pays three cents and each loss costs ninety-five. Win rate is the most misleading number in trading."
**Close:** "Always ask for profit per trade after fees."

## 3. "I tried to beat retail traders with their own data"
**Hook:** "Binance tells you exactly what retail traders are betting on. So I bet against them."
**Body:** "In 2022 it worked: up 24%. Then from 2024 on: down 11%. Hunting their stop-losses on Nasdaq lost money every single way I tried it."
**Close:** "The people who make money off retail aren't betting against them. They're charging them fees."

## 4. "195 strategies, zero passed"
**Hook:** "I gave an AI a thousand dollars of hypothetical money and one job: find a strategy that works."
**Body:** "Crypto, stocks, futures, sports betting, prediction markets, weather. 195 strategies. Every fee charged, tested on data it never saw. Zero passed. One football strategy showed a small edge and it's being paper-traded live right now."
**Close:** "Follow to see if it survives."

## 5. "The famous crypto trade that stopped working"
**Hook:** "This crypto trade used to be free money."
**Body:** "Buy Bitcoin, short the futures, collect the funding fee. 2021: up 20%. 2025: down 6% after costs. Big funds found it, and the edge disappeared."
**Close:** "If you heard about an edge on social media, assume it's already gone."

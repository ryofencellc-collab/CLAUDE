# Launch plan: sell the honest report (Track 3)

## The product

1. **The report** (`PRODUCT_REPORT.md`, export to PDF): "I Let an AI Test 198
   Trading Strategies." **$9.**
2. **Upsell, done-for-you strategy test: $49.** The buyer sends a strategy (a
   video link or written rules). We turn it into exact rules and run it
   through this exact pipeline: holdout, costs, null test. They get a 1-page
   verdict with the numbers. This is where AI has a real edge: a test that
   takes a person days takes this pipeline minutes.

## Why it can sell

- The audience is huge and already searching ("jj simon strategy", "does ORB
  work", "is this strategy profitable") and is being sold courses at
  $200–$2,000.
- We have something almost no one in that space has: **real, checkable
  numbers**, including the viral TikTok strategy itself.
- Being honest is the marketing hook: "I expected to find a money printer.
  Here's what 4.75 million bars of data said."

## What the owner does (AI can't)

1. Create a **Gumroad** (or Lemon Squeezy / Stan) account in your name and
   connect payouts. It's free, and they take a fee per sale.
2. Upload `PRODUCT_REPORT.md` exported as PDF and paste the listing below.
3. Post the content (scripts in `SCRIPTS.md`) on the TikTok account you
   already use, or a new one, and link the product in the bio.
4. For each $49 order, forward the strategy to me; I run it and write the
   verdict.

## Rules we won't break

No fake reviews or testimonials, no income claims, no impersonating anyone
(we name the strategy we tested, but we don't use the creator's branding or
imply endorsement), and a clear "not financial advice" note on everything.
